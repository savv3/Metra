{% blocktranslate trimmed %} These are the shortest times the Accounting Act allows, and Metra keeps everything after them as well. Other rules can require keeping material longer: the terms of a grant, the 10-year VAT adjustment period of a property investment, or an open tax audit, appeal or dispute. Check those before removing anything. Removing a year whose time has passed is not offered in Metra yet; when it is due, export the year first and decide with the board and the auditor. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Personal data in the books" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Vouchers, receipts and the invoices they rest on can name members and suppliers. They are bookkeeping material that the law requires to be kept, so they are kept for these periods even when a person asks to have their data erased (GDPR article 17(3)(b)). Changing or archiving a person or unit in the register does not change anything already booked: the books keep the names and amounts as they were booked. {% endblocktranslate %}
