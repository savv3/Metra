# Getting started

Metra keeps your register of households or other units and the people you
contact, collects readings, sends messages and newsletters, and makes
invoices.

## The menu

- **Units** and **People**: the register.
- **Readings**: reading rounds, such as the yearly water meter reading.
- **Invoices**: invoice runs, payments and reminders.
- **Send-outs**: messages, urgent alerts and newsletters, with their delivery status.
- **Settings** (admins): the organisation, unit types and fields, users and roles, message templates, invoice settings and backups.
- **Help**: these guides. Every page's Help link opens the guide for that page.

Your own language, the light or dark theme and two-factor login are in the
menu under your name.

## Setting up a new installation

The first admin to log in is taken through the **setup wizard**, one page at
a time:

1. **Organisation**: name, business ID, address and the public contact details.
2. **Language**: your own language. Everyone picks their own.
3. **Logo**: shown on the public page, members' pages, letters and invoices.
4. **Units and fields**: unit types and their fields. **Add the water association example** gives you households with property ID, meter number and other fields, and a yearly water meter.
5. **Email and SMS**: shows what is set up and sends you a test.
6. **Invoices**: bank account, payment terms, VAT and numbering.
7. **Invite the board**: each person gets an email with a link to choose a password.

Any step can be skipped, and nothing changes until a step is saved. Admins
can run the wizard again under **Settings > Run the setup wizard**.

## Members

Members never log in. They get a personal link by SMS, email or letter, where
they report their reading and correct their contact details. See
[Readings](/help/readings/).
