# Notifications

The bell at the top of every page, next to your name, shows how many
notifications you have not read yet. Click it to open the **Notifications**
page.

## What you are notified about

What you get depends on your role:

| Notification | Who gets it |
| --- | --- |
| A snapshot failed, or could not be sent offsite | Admins |
| The nightly snapshot was taken (off until you turn it on) | Admins |
| A snapshot was restored, or a restore failed | Admins |
| SMS or email in a send-out could not be delivered | Admins and the person who sent it |
| Members sent in readings or changes through their links | Admins and the board |
| Invoices became overdue (checked every morning) | Admins and the treasurer |
| A webhook could not be delivered (with the webhooks plugin) | Admins |

Plugins can add their own kinds.

Several of the same kind are joined into one so a busy day does not fill
the list: for example all changes members send in on one day, or all
messages of one send-out that could not be delivered. The notification
shows how many there are and becomes unread again when another one joins.

## Reading, marking and dismissing

The newest notifications are at the top and unread ones are highlighted.
Choose **Unread** to see only those.

- Click a notification's text to open the page it is about. It is then
  marked as read.
- **Mark as read** and **Mark as unread** change it without opening it.
- **Dismiss** removes it from your list. Others who got the same
  notification still have it.
- **Mark all as read** marks everything in your list as read.

Only you see your own notifications.

## Choosing what you get

Open **Notification settings** on the Notifications page, or in the menu
under your name. Tick the kinds you want and save. Only the kinds your role
can see are listed.

## Clean-up

Notifications you have read or dismissed are deleted after 90 days. Unread
ones are kept.
