# Readings

A reading round collects one reading per unit, for example everyone's water
meter in October.

## Reading types and rounds

An admin sets up what is read under **Settings > Readings > Reading types**: the unit
type it applies to, the unit of measure (such as m³), the decimals, and
whether it is a meter that keeps counting. Under **Readings**, open a round
(for example "2026") with the dates members may report in.

## Entering readings

The round page shows how many units have reported and which are missing. Type
values in row by row, or use **Enter** or **Correct** on a unit's page. For a
meter, the usage is the value minus the previous reading. A value lower than
last time, or far above the unit's usual usage, is saved but marked
**Check**. **Export as CSV** downloads the round.

## Members report their own readings

Members never get a password. Each household's contact gets a personal link
to a page, made for phones, where they enter the reading and correct their
own phone, email and address. Their changes are listed under **Readings >
Recent self-service changes**.

On the round page:

- **Send links to all units** sends each unit's main contact a new link on their preferred channel (email or SMS), or the other one if they have no address for it. Those with neither, or who prefer post, are listed for printed letters.
- **Send reminder to units missing a reading** does the same for those who have not reported.
- **Self-service links** prints letters with a QR code for everyone, for those missing a reading, or for those without phone or email.

A link for a round works until the round closes. Making a new link stops the
person's earlier one, and links can be stopped by hand.

The texts are under **Settings > Messages > Message templates**.
